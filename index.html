    Settings and credits

    Movies and actors

    Movie scenes and actor photos come from The Movie Database. It needs a free API key: create an account at themoviedb.org/settings/api, then paste either the API key or the read access token here. It is kept only in this browser.

    Where the pictures come from

    Anime characters and series are loaded from AniList. Movie scenes and actor photos are loaded from TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

    Obscura stores no pictures of its own. Every image is requested from those services while you play and remains the property of its rights holder. This is a fan-made quiz, not affiliated with any studio or publisher.
